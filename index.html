LESS REPETITION. MORE CLARITY.

Keep the work moving.
Keep the context intact.

Built for the operators moving between tools, the MSPs managing multiple customers, and the teams who need to know what happened next.

One action for your team.

Use Alerta’s single-alert acknowledgement. Zab-al-ack takes care of eligible Zabbix acknowledgements in the background, so operators can move on to the investigation.

Your boundaries, respected.

Choose the customers, Zabbix instances, triggers and hosts that participate. Every delivery checks the original problem and its approved scope before writing.

An outcome you can follow.

Keep a durable record of the request, delivery and result. Track pending, completed, uncertain or expired work, with a clear path to investigate failures.

A SMALL ACTION. A CAREFUL JOURNEY.

From your console
to the right source event.

Alerta stays the place your team works. Zab-al-ack handles the delivery behind it.

  1. 01

    Acknowledge in Alerta

    An authorised person acknowledges one eligible alert, using the familiar Alerta action.

  2. 02

    Capture and verify

    The signed request is saved with the acknowledgement. A worker verifies it and rechecks the original Zabbix problem.

  3. 03

    Record the outcome

    The worker delivers a bounded acknowledgement. The result is recorded and reflected back in Alerta.

When the outcome is uncertain, don’t guess. Reconciliation checks the source without blindly repeating a write. Already acknowledged? No extra acknowledgement or comment.

0.1.0a16 · PACKAGED ALPHA

A focused alpha.
An open path forward.

One authorised acknowledgement, one Alerta alert and one original Zabbix problem. a15 verified a14 and executed the three service suites it could not run, fixing an evidence writer that produced owner-only records. a16 reproduces and adopts the independent review of a15: no defect was found in the code, and five claims in the release prose are corrected — what the writer defect actually reached, which records the audit requires, which candidate a service run belongs to, and what the release verifier depends on. The illustrated guide, eight engineering upgrades and strict evidence checks remain. Bulk actions, chatops and multi-event alerts remain outside this release’s scope.
