# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss technical choices and evidence
without personal attacks. Welcome contributors regardless of experience,
identity or background. Harassment, threats, discriminatory behaviour and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation in
proportion to repeated or serious violations. They should explain the reason
privately where appropriate and permit an appeal to another maintainer.
Report concerns privately to the designated maintainers through the contact
route established when the public repository is launched. Do not post private
incident details in public issues. A private conduct-report contact must be
recorded alongside the maintainers before publication.
